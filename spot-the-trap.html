Spot the Trap

A short challenge. One decision worth practising.

A recruiter offers easy work. A caller says your savings are at risk. A website asks you to prove you are human.

Each situation asks for a decision. Spot the Trap gives people a place to practise that decision before it involves their own money or computer.

Read a made-up message, choose what you would do next, and see the details worth noticing. Each round takes about two minutes. There is no timer and no score to chase.

Challenges

  • 1. The job that asks for a deposit
    An unexpected chat message offers online work. You decide to hear more. Later, after completing a set of tasks, the recruiter sends another message.
  • 2. The call about a “safe account”
    A caller says they work for your bank. Your phone displays the bank's name. During the call, this message arrives.
  • 3. The human check that asks you to run something
    You follow a link to a document. Before it opens, a page displays these instructions.
  • 4. The delivery update that needs checking
    You are expecting a parcel today. A delivery update arrives by text.

How to play

  1. Pick a challenge and read the situation.
  2. Choose your next step.
  3. Select “See the explanation.”
  4. Read the feedback and the numbered details.
  5. Take away one useful rule. Share the challenge if someone you know might find it helpful.

Some examples contain a clear scam request. Others look ordinary and need checking. You do not have to identify a fraudster from a message alone; you can choose a sensible next step while something remains uncertain.

Your selected answer stays on this page. It is not included in a shared link or card. You do not need an account. See the privacy page for information about hosting and request metadata.

Have a real message you are worried about? The message checker can look for specific warning patterns. It cannot confirm the sender or guarantee that a request is genuine.
