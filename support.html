Keep it free, and keep it honest

SaveSaveSaveSave has no accounts, sells no data and never shows what you scan to advertisers. If it helped you, this is how you can help it.

We will never message you asking for crypto.

This page is the only place we ask. If anyone contacts you claiming to collect donations for SaveSaveSaveSave, it is a scam.

01Where the money goes

Running costs: the domain, Cloudflare, and the security data the scanner reads. And time: building new checks and writing the guides.

A donation is a gift. It does not buy a better verdict, a listing, an endorsement, or any influence over what the scanner says about anything.
